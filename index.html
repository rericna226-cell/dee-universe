DEE Universe · Kernel v27

One local rule. A three-dimensional world. Behaviors that appear over time.

DEE is a three-dimensional cellular automaton with internal state. Each position reads its local surroundings and all positions evolve step by step under the same dynamics. The project studies what organizations, forms of processing and effective physical descriptions can arise from that evolution.

This site separates internal DEE results, mathematical constructions and hypotheses of correspondence with our universe. Understanding the mechanism comes before assigning physical meaning.

Understand the world first. Then ask what can emerge.

DEE does not begin by inserting particles, memory or known physical equations. It begins with positions, local state and an evolution rule.

1

A 3D space

The DEE laboratory is a finite three-dimensional lattice of discrete positions.

2

Each position has state

A position stores internal information. A binary occupied/empty image does not represent the full microstate.

3

Interaction is local

Each update uses information from the position itself and its immediate surroundings. No central controller writes the global result.

4

Time advances in ticks

The same dynamics is applied repeatedly. Repetition produces the complete history of the system.

DEE is first built, executed and measured. Only then do we ask which mathematical formalism correctly describes a sector. Known physics is not inserted as the answer in advance.

Open hypothesis
Does DEE already “prove” quantum mechanics?
No. The v27 corpus develops quantum representations and sector constructions in defined domains. Identification with the quantum physics of our universe is an additional correspondence question.
Was the Schrödinger equation programmed into the kernel?
No. In reversible sectors a unitary representation is built and, under stated conditions, a generator and Schrödinger forms are constructed. The equation belongs to the representation of the sector, not to a microscopic instruction inserted as a target.
Does DEE prove gravity or general relativity?
No. Effective relativistic and geometric descriptions are developed under premises. An internal geometric response is not automatically universal gravitation and does not by itself fix the physical constants of our universe.
